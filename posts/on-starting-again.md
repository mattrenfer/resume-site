Starting again is its own skill, separate from starting.

The first time, you don't know enough to be intimidated. The second time you know
exactly what it costs, which is why most people don't. You've seen the gap between
the thing in your head and the thing on the page, and you remember it being wide.

So you start small. A paragraph that doesn't have to be good. A file with a date on
it. You let the work be ordinary for a while, because ordinary is what compounds.

This is me letting it be ordinary.
