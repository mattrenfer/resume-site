I saw you
your knowing smile
relaxed posture
moving gracefully
comfortable being
living out loud

I saw you
on Maine beach days
diving into frigid waves
sweet conversations
“chattin’ it up,” you’d say
laughing heartily
getting to the heart of the matter

I saw you
sitting cross legged
teaching yoga poses
belting out a new song lyric
rockin’ to Melissa Etheridge
who “electrified and rocked you”

I saw you
at a peaceful calm
with a lightness of spirit
dancing spontaneously
feeling fully
mourning a loss

I saw you
wise and bold
like the owls you loved
a steady strength
speaking calm convictions
giving radical permission
to simply be

I saw you
cookin’ LOBSTAH
cozying up to a book
re-watching rom coms
“much needed downtime...”
calming stormy waters

I saw you
in full color
a prism
drawing in the world’s light
radiating through
an embodied reminder to...

“BREATHE”

your license plate
your living mantra

in
out

you were real
clear-eyed
accepting
a reassuring presence
radiant, genuine, grounded, curious
a non-judging confidant

you gave all of yourself
your attention, your presence
a rare glimpse of what it is to be
otherworldly human

I saw you
allowing yourself to be seen
loving yourself
loving the world
