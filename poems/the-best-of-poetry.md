the best of Poetry is: 
Honest
Wrestles in the Incomplete
neither trite; nor pat
not always sweet 
more like...

Jazz 
than anything neat.

Pokes.
and Prods.
in Meter and 
Rhyme
takes its time, Travels 
places 
times 
deep 
wide

unafraid to— 
Piddle, Dabble
in Crafted Refrains that don't 
bloviate, babble.

offers up a Thought for the taking
an Invitation
stirring us to 
Awaken
waves to the shore, a moving force, an Authored Creation 
with a Voice.

binds Me-to-Me
Us-to-Us, to Our
beating Heart, Our
humanity, Our—
divine spark.
