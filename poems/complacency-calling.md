Happy Birthday to US
we made it this far
we carried the torch of liberty
but not without a permanent scar
made strides toward a more perfect union
bent toward the arc of justice
but if it made you at all uncomfortable, well,
Heed Complacency's Calling.

let the lulls of the powerful soothe you...

"take it from us, it's just fine not to care
to look only after yourself and your people
to not hear; to not dare."

"in fact, don't feel anything at all
beyond pride for success
enjoy the priviledge we bargained
no need for distress."

"just keep your head in the sand
as we roll back, rewrite and re-brand
the history of human progress
and marvel at the profits we're making
the Dow's at 50,000."

"listen in to our program
of un-ending outrage
hear the claims of unfairness
as if it were cool to be unhinged
after all, this is your land and your rightful right
with an undeserving neighbor who just wants to take it."

"strive for the ease
of conformity and control
join us in building a monoculture
the Tower of Babel
of the new status quo."

"don't ask the honest questions
the hard look in our memory and mirror..."

what good is any measure of success
if we encourage hate for one another?

what good are the powerfully rich
if they trickle down a model for blame and for fear?

the false promise of complacency
is that it never fulfills
it's only good at keeping you content
until your own will no longer wills

no, sir, I reject your petty coffer
as more than a mere harmless offer
to be complacent is to be complicit
you've done enough talking
let's give the mic to someone who cares
someone who doesn't stir the pot with division and lies
it's past time to judge you
you, yourself, on your own merit
to hold you to account
beyond any compare
you've failed in your own promise
of deliverance in a life better under the sun

let's redefine what "winning" really is
as beyond what we earn
it's more like what we share in
more about what we learn

see, I was naive
here I was thinking we were doing the whole
"love one another" thing
I thought we learned the lesson of history
that winning doesn't come at the cost of another
it costs us only being true
true to what we said we'd do
true to our fellow man
to extend the hand of human dignity
and yet we have hope and possibility
so long as we stand shoulder-to-shoulder
apart from this, we are broken
apart from this, we grow older
but not wiser
prideful and colder
when we turn toward a sister and brother
we find ourselves whole-er and hol-i-er
we find perfection in progress
in a posture of kindness
far beyond any painlessness
broken promises of un-mess
we can't wish this away so easily
can't make it right by not seeing
can't heal from our wounds
by turning away from one another
that'd be outright betrayal

and maybe the world changed
just a bit too fast
but it doesn't have to mean
we have to go so far back
needing a change doesn't mean
we burn it all down to the ground
without anything keeping us grounded in between
it doesn't have to mean
it's okay to be mean
we can simply recognize
we all have a need to be seen

it ain't about dolin' out
heeps of white guilt
you just can't heal from a thing
without naming it first

you confused more rights for all
with less rights for you
and if this made you insecure, well,
it says more about you
it says more about you
than it does about we
we know that we've strayed
when the conclusions we draw
become a problem of caring
of thinking of others at all
become a problem of too much empathy
dismissed too easily
as weak sentimentality

the life and the promise
is either for us—all of us—or it isn't
which one is it, America?
who gets in, who doesn't?
who gets it, who doesn't?
if you thought this time was it
yeah, it wasn't
this was another shameful act
in a tragedy of errors
no comedic value to be found
in our fits and our flails

how many do we deport?
prevent to abort?
until we're sufficiently "Christian"?
sufficiently "us"?
we're getting it twisted
"woke" used to mean historically aware
now its a punching bag
as if being aware meant being unfair

let it disappoint you to seek to do better
to reach for higher fruit
than low hanging juice
Kool-aid of the cult
who've convinced themselves
that sweet poison which goes down smooth
is not bad for you

can't help what we feel and see with our own eyes
if we're getting to be so great
why does it feel not at all wise?
why does it feel like it was all a lie?

we traded good faith engagement for cheap entertainment
civil responsibility for painless co-existence
educated citizenry with misinformation
seeking hard after truth for hot take opinions
and if you were to ask me if this came at too high a cost
of course, yes, just look at what we've lost
the common good took a hit
decency is there, but it ain't hip
we're less grounded
more unmoored
less patient with each other
relationships scorned

why isn't it obvious to see
the harm we do to one another
through bigotry, patriarchy
is the harm we do to us, too?
why the choice to confine
pre-define, pre-determine and hate?
when there's a chance to demonstrate
"All God's children" as a value to hold
self-evident truths
none better foretold

so join me brothers and sisters
in the battle not against flesh and blood
but the powers that be
those that would lead us away from a united "we"
to the self-defeating trap of complacency
the world of difference we make
in a world of difference is what makes us great
let's aim to be real in the fight for what's real
so much more than "blood and soil"
we're a nation of ideals
