---
title: Sustained Flame
date: 2026-08-19
tags:
  - poetry
excerpt:
---
As guided spark lights the dark, 
sogged torch fights the fog,
encircled fire melts icy ire,
a sustained flame
prevents going insane.

True now as it was back when
the highwire, dire surival
depends on dancing,
without and within,
in sway, in the way,
in romancing,
with shifting wind.
