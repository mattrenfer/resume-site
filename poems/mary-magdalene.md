title: Mary Magdalene
date: 2026-08-05

---
What's behind your expression?
your Mona Lisa smile
was it that your soul was transformed
when He offered to walk and talk
and stay awhile?

What did Jesus tell you?
what secrets do you know?
what did the disciples miss?
to go where one must go
unknown unknowns.

Was it disheartening
to be blamed,
sent away, removed,
envied, treated rude?

Still you stayed
when everyone strayed
still you were true
on that third day
without any ado.

It's clear what He did not do
He did not withhold from you
and who are we to refuse
the one He told to?
