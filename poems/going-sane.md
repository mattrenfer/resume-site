title: going sane

certain times find
to try the sane thing
un-reasoning
looks like cracking, wholly feeling
going in-and-out reaching
self-emptying.

feeling we find
in hidden disguise
common sense
betraying innocence.

moving on.
concrete roads.
easy streets.
stark categories.
stock market trades.
the transactional phrase.
ways and means, means and ways.
are not all that there is
any number of days.

looking we see
a glimpse of the question:
now, how to be?
adhere to this wholly?
give the sum of all parts away?
all or nothing?
no in between?

what of the tears of things?
things not obvious,
inner pull gravity.
what of beauty?
the chorus of strings
the pure song in each of us
colored melodies.

what of that calling; that energy?

if this be insanity
judged harshly
with easy parody
call us the un-reasonably sane
artists of the day
genuine human charity
granting permission 
to be.

look! closely—
there's no easy divide
of unholy/holy
all's already here
in the ordinary
imperfectly
all are becoming
in our simple choosing
to see and be seen
