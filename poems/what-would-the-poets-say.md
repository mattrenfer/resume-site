---
title: What Would the Poets Say
date: 2026-07-19
tags:
  - poetry
excerpt:
---

In a time like no other
I have often wondered
what the poets would tell us with the fullest expression
would they urge us to expand, lest we lessen?
beckon us self-examine, to question?

Would they say the world was too much with us?
to stay gold and take heart with what is ours
if only we could keep our head
to be masters of our fate

Or to rage, rage against the dying of the light
to not go gentle into that good night
that the world would end in fire or in ice?

Would they warn us of our dream deferred?
or that hope is the thing with feathers
the freedom song of the caged bird?

Would they advise us to take the road less traveled?
in our crossroad, in our choice, to where we're gathered?
define what is and what could be
to give us your tired, your poor
the refuge?

That the powerful play goes on and on
to hear the repeated refrain
"still I rise"
had we but world enough, and time, to decide
choose to heed what was wise

Would they turn to hear what we would say?
as poets of this new dawn
this new day
since feeling is first
that we may contribute a verse
in this beautifully, tragically powerful play.
