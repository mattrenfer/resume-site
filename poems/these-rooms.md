title: These Rooms
date: 2026-08-09

These rooms come in many shapes,
holding water, thin escape,
Fluid in its taking form,
drowning in becoming born.

Squaring solid sour grapes,
turnkey triangle twisted fate,
hardened heart, misshaped face,
sunken ship, anchored place.

First the oblong concave frame,
dimensions, angles,
trimmed and tamed.

Next an honest time of sharing,
Asymmetrical mirrored pairing.

Now a final circle forms,
recited prayer, though never worn,
asking granted knowing difference,
between halfway in;
and surrendered deliverance.
