been around, I guess
big small towns
little big stress
any town how
anywhere when
came and went there
where here is again

local hero weirdo
haircut concierto
agnostic punks
self-described monks

low places bars
high society
average guy cars
same variety

been around, I guess
different in here, different in there
same as in that, same as in this
difference in same, same in difference
