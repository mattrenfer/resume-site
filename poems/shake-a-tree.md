shake a tree
none too tall
none too big
none too small
just the right size
for where you are
from where you stand
without overreach
without demand.

what fruit falls down?
are you able to eat?
bitter, sour, sweet?
feed yourself?
prepare a feast?
look to the East?

where the sun and land meet
where you can see
a possibility
a chance for today
a chance to be.

today, it’s okay
to shake a tree.
